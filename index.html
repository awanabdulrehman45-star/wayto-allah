FAQs

Common questions

Who can join?

Children and adults, male and female students, from any country.

How long and how often are classes?

Normally five days a week, 30–40 minutes per class, depending on the course.

Which platform are classes on?

Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams. You will see the platform and a Join button in your dashboard.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. One trial class per new student, with no payment required.

Can I choose my class timings?

Yes. You request preferred days and times, and the academy confirms once a teacher approves.

Which languages does the website support?

English, Urdu and Arabic.
